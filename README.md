## About me

I am a mechanical engineer and control-systems researcher working at the intersection of **robotics, nonlinear control, safety-critical control, and machine learning**. My research focuses on designing reliable controllers for autonomous mechanical systems, particularly quadrotor UAVs and wheeled mobile robots.

I hold an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). My work has appeared in *Robotics and Autonomous Systems*, *ISA Transactions*, and the International Conference on Robotics and Mechatronics (ICRoM).

## Research interests

- Nonlinear, sliding-mode, prescribed-time, and fault-tolerant control
- Control Barrier Functions and safety-critical robotic navigation
- Autonomous quadrotors and wheeled mobile robots
- Machine learning for dynamical-system modeling
- Deep learning, reinforcement learning, and nature-inspired optimization
